5. Real-time sync NEW

What changed: Previously sync ran every 15 minutes. Now any queued offline op fires a one-shot WorkRequest with a CONNECTED constraint — runs the moment network is available. Effectively real-time.
flowchart TD Submit["📥 pending_sync.add()"] --> Trigger["⚡ SyncTrigger.fire()

Why it matters for the audit: Coords are captured at the moment of the action and travel with the queued payload — so a sample distribution recorded at Dr. X's clinic at 11am still shows the clinic's coordinates even if it actually syncs at 4pm from a different location.
